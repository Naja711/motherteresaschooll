School Motto

"Let Your Light Shine"

At our school, we believe that every child carries a unique light within—a light of goodness, talent, intelligence, and character. Guided by our motto, "Let Your Light Shine," we strive to nurture academic excellence along with integrity, discipline, leadership, and respect for others.

We encourage our students to discover their gifts, work with dedication, and grow into confident and compassionate individuals who serve society with responsibility and love.

Inspired by the words of Mother Teresa, "Not all of us can do great things, but we can do small things with great love," we remind our students that true success is not only in achievement but in using their knowledge and talents to make a positive difference in the world.
